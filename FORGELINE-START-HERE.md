- **Editing guide:** `forgeline/docs/EDITING-GUIDE.md`
- **Validation:** `forgeline/docs/VALIDATION.md`

Run `npm start` from `forgeline/` to resume the saved local site. The first five navigation pages satisfy the assignment; eight additional detail/privacy pages complete the content and link journeys.

The site uses Hubsteel as its parent theme. Its pages, shared header/footer, and reusable sections are editable in Elementor. Enquiries are stored privately in WordPress. Company details and projects are clearly identified as fictional coursework examples; email notifications are off until configured.
